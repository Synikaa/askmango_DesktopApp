# Ask Mango Desktop

Desktop app for [Ask Mango](https://askmango.app): a friend that lives on your screen. Hold two keys, ask out loud, and Mango answers in a warm voice while it points, circles and draws right on your screen.

## Download

Head over to the [Releases page](https://github.com/Synikaa/askmango_DesktopApp/releases) to download the latest version for your computer, or get it from [askmango.app](https://askmango.app).

## Available Platforms

- **macOS** (`.dmg`, Apple silicon: M1 or later)
- **Windows** (`.exe` installer)

## Installation

### macOS

1. Download `AskMango-Mac.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Synikaa/askmango_DesktopApp/releases/latest)
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag Ask Mango to your Applications folder
3. Launch Ask Mango from Applications and allow the microphone, screen recording and accessibility permissions it asks for

### Windows

1. Download `AskMango-Setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Synikaa/askmango_DesktopApp/releases/latest)
2. Run the installer
3. Launch Ask Mango from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut

## Getting Started

1. Open the app and sign in with your Ask Mango account (Google or a code by email; it's free to start)
2. Follow the short welcome tour
3. In any app, hold **Control + Option** (Mac) or **Ctrl + Alt** (Windows), ask your question out loud, and let go

Ask Mango updates itself: a new version downloads in the background and installs when you restart.

## Requirements

- An [Ask Mango](https://askmango.app) account (free to start)
- macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or later), or Windows 10 or 11
- Microphone access
